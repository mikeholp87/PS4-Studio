# Code of Conduct

We want a harassment-free, respectful community for everyone regardless of experience, identity, or background.

**Expected:** be kind and constructive; assume good faith; give and accept feedback gracefully; focus on what is best for the project and its users.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, discrimination, doxxing, sexualised attention, sustained disruption, and requests for help with piracy, exploits against other people's devices, or account abuse.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that violate this code. Report concerns privately via the contact in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) (use "conduct" in the subject).

This is a short adaptation in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
